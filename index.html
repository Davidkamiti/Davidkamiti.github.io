Credit Risk Prediction

A Random Forest that flags risky loans with a ROC-AUC of 0.91, deployed as a web app.

At a 0.6 cut-off the model is right 81% of the time when it flags a likely defaulter and catches 71% of real defaulters. I balanced the classes with SMOTE, packaged the scaler and model together, and wrote up the weaknesses I found in my own evaluation.

Python, scikit-learn, imbalanced-learn, Streamlit

Telecom Customer Churn

Customers on an international plan leave four times as often, and trouble with support predicts who goes next.

I compared six model types and tuned XGBoost for F1 on the churn class, not accuracy. On held-out data it identifies 95 of 121 churners at 84% precision. The threshold was chosen on training data only.

Python, XGBoost, scikit-learn, cross-validation

Hacker News Hiring Posts

Employers name SQL in only 4% of posts, and LLM mentions stayed flat across a year.

I collected 3,691 job posts through an API, extracted about 40 skills with pattern matching, and tracked demand month by month. I checked my matcher against an independent count and fixed a flawed rule that was flagging company names.

Python, pandas, text mining, matplotlib
